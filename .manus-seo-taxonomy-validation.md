# SEO Feed and IAM Taxonomy Validation

The live sitemap, RSS feed, and robots response were verified with the local server. The Blog filter exposes the refined IAM taxonomy. Selecting **IGA Architecture** reduced the list to the multi-source IGA article, whose visible category label matches the selected filter.

The remaining refined categories were exercised against the live Blog controls. **Identity Data Governance** and **Identity Correlation** returned the IGA article; **SailPoint IdentityIQ**, **Microsoft Entra ID**, and **Lifecycle Provisioning** returned one integration article each. The automated taxonomy mapping test explicitly maps the first three categories to the IGA article and the final three to the Entra ID × SailPoint IIQ article.
